FAQ

What should buyers confirm?

Clear application and sample information helps us discuss the correct product direction.

What elastic webbing does Jialun manufacture?

Our range includes thick elastic, premium elastic, tank elastic, sesame elastic, children's shoe elastic, hollow webbing, gold-thread elastic, silver-thread elastic and twill elastic.

What widths are available?

Widths from 0.8 to 50cm can be discussed. Common widths include 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 8 and 10cm.

Can colors be customized?

Yes. For colors beyond black, brown and white, please provide a physical sample for matching.

What materials are used?

The main materials are polyester and latex. The exact construction is confirmed according to the product and approved sample.

Where is the elastic webbing used?

Our elastic webbing is mainly developed for footwear and pet products, including flexible connections, support, wrapping and adjustment structures.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum order requirements are confirmed according to specification and quantity.
