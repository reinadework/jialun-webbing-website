FAQ

What should buyers confirm?

Clear application and sample information helps us discuss the correct product direction.

What widths are available?

Widths from 0.8 to 50cm can be discussed. Common widths include 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 8 and 10cm.

Can colors be customized?

Yes. For colors beyond black, brown and white, please provide a physical sample for matching.

What materials are used?

The main materials are polyester and latex. The exact construction is confirmed by product and sample.

What is the minimum order?

Minimum order requirements are confirmed according to specification and quantity.
